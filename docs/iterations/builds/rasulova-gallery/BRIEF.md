## Brief, before planning
1. Vibe: luminous, sculptural, personal, tactile. Authored reference: a contemporary small-object exhibition, not a shop template.
2. Journey: encounter a real vase, understand the handmade surface, explore objects, assemble a personal exhibition, see the atelier, prepare an inquiry.
3. Energy: spacious opening, quiet statement, expressive collection peak, intimate workshop, composed ending.
4. Feeling curve: curiosity through the opening still life; calm through the short statement; delight through the personal exhibition; intimacy through workshop photographs; resolve through a downloadable inquiry.
5. Signature: select objects into a miniature exhibition shelf, which becomes a portable inquiry document. No invented prices, specifications or stock.
6. Aesthetic: light editorial gallery, white/chalk, graphite and wine red. Serif display is appropriate to the actual sculptural art portfolio; sans for labels.
7. Distinct gallery rooms, natural vertical flow. No continuous world, no long empty pinning.
8. Assets: supplied object and workshop JPG photographs. No generated imagery. Marketing photos contain baked labels: preserve originals in detail view, prefer workshop photos in the opening.
